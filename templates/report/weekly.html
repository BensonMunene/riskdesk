Standing rules

  • A name is bought when it is in a long-term uptrend (above its {{ r.meta.rule.long_ma }}-day average), sold short only in a confirmed downtrend, and closed when the signal reverses by more than {{ r.meta.rule.hysteresis|pct:0 }}.
  • Position size falls automatically when a name's volatility rises, so one wild stock cannot dominate the book, and no name may carry more than {{ r.meta.mandate.max_risk_share|pct:0 }} of its risk.
  • Earnings within two weeks are flagged; the client may prefer to halve the position into the print.
  • If the one-day VaR exceeds {{ r.meta.mandate.var_99_1d_max|pct }} of capital or any limit above breaches, the book is scaled down the same day.

Method, in brief

{{ r.meta.horizon }}

Returns and volatility. Daily price returns on dividend-adjusted closes; volatility is the standard deviation scaled to a year, weighting recent days more (half-life {{ r.meta.halflife }} days).

Correlation and risk shares. From the same {{ r.meta.lookback }}-day window. A name's risk share is its weight times its beta to the book, so shares add to 100%.

VaR / ES. Historical: the 1st percentile of the book's daily pro-forma returns over the window; ES is the average beyond it. One-week figures scale by √5.

Factors. Each name regressed on eleven ETF-built factors (market, size, value, momentum, quality, low-vol, rates, credit, dollar, gold, oil).

Stress. Historical replays apply each name's actual return over the episode to today's weights; hypothetical shocks go through the factor betas.

Objectives. Solved with convex optimisation on the six-month covariance. {{ r.construction.mu_note }}

House rule. {{ r.meta.rule.description }} Longs are capped at {{ r.meta.mandate.max_long|pct:0 }}, shorts at {{ r.meta.mandate.max_short|pct:0 }}, gross at {{ r.meta.mandate.gross_max|pct:0 }}, weekly turnover at {{ r.meta.mandate.turnover_max_weekly|pct:0 }}.

Glossary

Gross / net exposure. Longs plus shorts / longs minus shorts, as a share of capital.

Beta. How much the book moves for a 1% move in {{ r.meta.benchmark }}.

Volatility. The typical size of a yearly swing; divide by 16 for a daily feel.

VaR 99%. The daily loss exceeded only one day in a hundred. ES is the average loss on those days.

Risk share. A name's fraction of total book volatility.

Effective number of bets. How many independent bets a correlated set of names behaves like.

Sharpe. Return above cash per unit of volatility; above 1 is good.

Max drawdown. The worst peak-to-trough fall.

Pro forma. Today's weights replayed over the past; describes the current book, not a track record.

RSI. A 0-100 momentum gauge; above 70 stretched up, below 30 stretched down.
