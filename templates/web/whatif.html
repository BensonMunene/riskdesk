How the CIO uses this page

  1. Type the trades you are considering (shares, dollars, or weight).
  2. Analyse shows the full before/after: vol, VaR, beta, factor tilts, sector mix, risk contributions, stress losses, and every mandate limit.
  3. A red verdict means the trade would breach a limit; amber means risk rises more than 10%.
  4. Save as proposal records it for review; approved proposals can be booked into the live positions.
Tip: the Optimiser and Position sizing pages can send their trade lists straight here.
