Three sizing questions this page answers

  • How big should the whole book be? Vol targeting scales gross exposure so that expected volatility hits the target.
  • Is risk spread sensibly across names? Equal risk contribution reallocates the equity sleeve so each name contributes the same volatility.
  • How much of a new idea can I add? Enter a ticker and a risk budget; the tool solves for the weight that delivers exactly that share of portfolio vol, accounting for correlation with everything already held.
